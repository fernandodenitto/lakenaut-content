Genie Agents, formerly called Genie spaces, let business users ask questions in plain language over a small set of governed tables. Genie writes read-only SQL, runs it with the asking user's own permissions, and shows the query next to the answer.

Read the four concepts in order: what an agent is and who can do what with it, how authors tune it (knowledge store, instructions, trusted assets), how to measure it (benchmarks, feedback, monitoring), and how to reach it from outside its own chat window (API, embedding, Slack and Teams, MCP, Supervisor Agent).

Two neighbours share the name: **Genie One** is the business-user home that routes questions to agents, and **Genie Code** is the coding assistant formerly called Databricks Assistant.
